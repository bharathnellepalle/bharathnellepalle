### About

I am an open-source software consultant and developer. I advise on open-source license compliance and dependency transparency — scanning codebases, identifying licenses and copyrights, assessing obligations, and producing accurate SBOMs and attribution notices.

As a developer, I build the tooling that makes this work repeatable and scalable. My current focus is extending governance practices to AI — applying the same rigour used for open-source components to AI models, datasets and their usage terms.

| Area | What it involves |
